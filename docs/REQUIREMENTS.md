# Requirements

Requirements are accepted product commitments. Assign immutable IDs and do not reuse retired IDs.

| ID | Requirement and acceptance criteria | Priority | Source/owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `FR-001` | _Replace with a testable functional requirement and observable acceptance criteria._ | Must | _Owner_ | Proposed |

Use `FR` for functional, `NFR` for quality attributes, `SEC` for security/privacy and `DATA` for data requirements. Split requirements that need different acceptance evidence.
